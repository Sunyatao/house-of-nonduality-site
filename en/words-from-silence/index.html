What is it?

Words from Silence began as a series of quiet evenings with poetry, short texts, silence and reflection around mysticism, contemplation and nonduality. This form may return, but no longer on a fixed monthly rhythm.

What do we read?

We read texts from different mystical, poetic and contemplative traditions: poems, short fragments and quotations that invite slowing down, attention and inner silence.

What can you expect?

The evening consists of a simple alternation of text, silence and conversation. Sometimes there is a short introduction, sometimes a brief exchange, sometimes mostly silence.

Practical

  • No fixed rhythm
  • The date, time and location will be announced for each gathering
  • Dutch and English, depending on the texts and who is present
