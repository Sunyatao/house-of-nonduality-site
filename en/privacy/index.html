Data & privacy

Privacy

When you contact The House of Nonduality or register for a gathering through this website, you share a small amount of personal information. Below you can read how that information is handled.

What information is collected?

Through the forms on this website, the following information may be received: your name, email address, message, selected gathering or gatherings, number of people and any notes you choose to include.

What is this information used for?

This information is only used to reply to your message, follow up on your registration, send practical information about a gathering or contact you when needed.

Forms

The forms on this website are technically processed through Formspree. This means that the information you submit through a form is also processed by Formspree in order to deliver the message.

Newsletter

When you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is used to send you news and updates from The House of Nonduality. You can unsubscribe at any time through the link at the bottom of the newsletter.

Is information shared?

Your information is not sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. It is only used for the functioning of The House of Nonduality and communication around gatherings.

How long is information kept?

Information is not kept longer than necessary for the communication, registration or practical follow-up for which it was shared.

Requesting changes or deletion

If you would like to view, correct or delete your information, you can write to contact@houseofnonduality.com .
