About the House

A place for silence, contemplation, openness and encounter.

A house for silence and insight

The House of Nonduality is a growing place for silence, contemplation, encounter and the question of what is real. Through gatherings, texts and conversations, it creates space to look more deeply into experience, presence and consciousness. Alongside the gatherings, a collection of voices and insights from different contemplative, mystical and philosophical traditions is also taking shape — not as a final word, but as an invitation to look further.

What does nonduality mean?

Nonduality points to the insight that reality is less divided than it may first appear: not a separate “I” over here, facing a world out there. In simple words: nonduality invites us to explore whether we are really as separate as we think.

How does this take form?

The House takes shape through gatherings, contemplative texts, shared meals, conversation, poetry, meditation and a growing collection of sources. At its heart is a simple movement: to slow down, to listen, and to look attentively at what is present.

Why a house?

A house is a place of arrival. You do not have to prove anything there. You enter, take a seat, listen, eat, read, write, reflect, or follow a thread that leads you deeper within. The website follows that same logic: each room opens a different doorway into silence, contemplation, and the question of what is real.

Not to get somewhere else, but to see more clearly what is already here.
