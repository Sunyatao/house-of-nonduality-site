Gatherings

The House takes shape anew whenever content, place and moment come together.

No fixed programme

The House does not take shape through a fixed calendar. Sometimes a talk arises around a text or theme; at other times, a longer walk in nature, an evening of poetry or a day with silence, conversation and shared food. A gathering takes place when there is genuine space and a fitting occasion for it.

A possible form

Talks and inquiry

An in-depth exploration of a spiritual text, a theme within nonduality or a question calling for closer attention — with room for explanation, silence and conversation.

A possible form

Walks in nature

Longer walks in quieter, wilder landscapes. A poem, meditation or short talk may serve as an anchor, alongside room to walk freely and simply be together.

A possible form

Days together

Small-scale days with a few shared anchors: arrival, a talk, silence and conversation. Sometimes accompanied by a shared picnic or potluck, without every moment needing to be structured.

A possible form

Poetry and texts

An evening around one or several powerful poems or mystical texts, interwoven with silence, interpretation, personal reflection and conversation.

When something takes shape

New gatherings do not follow a fixed rhythm. The newsletter lets you know when a talk, walk or other encounter takes shape.
