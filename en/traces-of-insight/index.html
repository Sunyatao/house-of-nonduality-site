Traces of Insight

From books, texts and sources, traces of insight slowly grow here: notes, quotations and connections around nonduality, mysticism, philosophy and contemplation.

A living library

This space begins as a library: a collection of quotes, notes, texts and sources. But what grows here are mostly insights — living traces that branch, deepen and become connected with one another.

A digital garden

The page will slowly grow into a digital garden of quotations, notes, concepts, examples and references. Not as a finished system, but as an organic network of inquiry and contemplation.

Paths through the whole

Later, some insights will be gathered into paths: clear routes through the material, as if looking out from the library and seeing different ways leading toward the mountain.

For now

This space is still in progress. Until it opens further, you can join a gathering, subscribe to the newsletter or get in touch if there is a text, source or trace you would like to share.
